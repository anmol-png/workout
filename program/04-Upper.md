# Day 4 — UPPER (Second Chest · Back · Delt · Arm Dose)

**Target time:** ~55 min · **Working sets:** 19 · **Coverage:** chest 6, back 6, side delts 3, biceps 2, triceps 2

> **This session exists to give every upper-body muscle its second weekly stimulus.** Push and Pull
> did the heavy, low-rep work. This day is **deliberately hypertrophy-biased** — more machines,
> more cables, higher reps, closer to failure, less spinal and joint stress.

> **Every exercise here is different from Push and Pull.** Dip instead of bench, pulldown instead
> of pull-up, machine instead of free weight. Same muscles, different resistance profiles and joint
> angles — which covers regions a single exercise misses and gives your joints a break from
> repeating the identical groove twice a week.

> Read [00-Program-Overview.md](00-Program-Overview.md) first.

## A. Weighted Dip — *lower chest and triceps, in a deep stretch*
**3 sets × 6–10 reps · RPE 8 · rest 2 min · Start: bodyweight**

> **If dips bother your shoulders, or you can't do 6 clean reps:** use **Incline Barbell Press**
> at 3 × 6–10 @ RPE 8 instead. Assisted dip machine also works.

- **Why the dip:** it loads the chest and triceps together through a **very deep stretched
  position** — deeper than a bench press allows. That stretched-position loading is a potent
  hypertrophy stimulus and it's the main reason this is here rather than a second bench variation.
- **Why 6–10 @ RPE 8:** it's a heavy compound and you're supporting your full bodyweight. Staying 2
  reps shy protects the shoulder, which is the joint most at risk in a deep dip.
- **Cues:** lean the torso forward ~15–30° to bias the chest (staying bolt upright biases triceps).
  Elbows track back, not flared wide. Descend until the upper arm is roughly parallel to the floor
  — **stop there**, going deeper stresses the shoulder capsule without adding stimulus. Control the
  descent, don't drop.
- **Progression:** 3×10 clean → add 2.5 kg on a dip belt, restart at 6.

## B. Lat Pulldown, wide pronated grip — *lats, second vertical pull*
**3 sets × 8–12 reps · RPE 8 · rest 2 min · Start: calibrate**

- **Why a pulldown when Tuesday had pull-ups:** same movement pattern, different execution. The
  pulldown lets you work in a **higher rep range with precisely controlled load** — you can't
  easily do a clean set of 12 weighted pull-ups, but you can do 12 quality pulldowns. It's also
  fixed and supported, so you can push harder without your grip or core failing first.
- **Why wide pronated:** biases the upper lats and teres major slightly more than the narrower
  grip used on pull-ups. Different emphasis, same session slot.
- **Cues:** chest up, slight backward lean (~15°, held constant — don't turn it into a row). Pull
  the bar to your upper chest by driving the **elbows down**, not by pulling with the hands.
  Control the return all the way to a full stretch with the shoulder blades rising.
- **Progression:** 3×12 @ RPE ≤ 8 → next notch, restart at 8.

## C. Superset — *chest + back*
Do **C1 → 20 s → C2 → rest 90 s → repeat. 3 rounds.** Antagonist pairing — pressing and pulling
don't interfere, and alternating them lets each recover while the other works. Saves ~5 min.

### C1. Machine Chest Press (or Pec Deck Fly) — *chest, safe near-failure*
**3 × 10–15 · RPE 9 · Start: calibrate**
- **Why a machine:** you already did the technical free-weight pressing on Monday. A machine is
  stabilized, so **all** the effort goes into the chest rather than into balancing a load, and you
  can safely take the set to RPE 9 with no spotter and no risk. That combination — high effort,
  low risk, low skill cost — is exactly what a second weekly dose should be.
- **Pec deck alternative:** if you want more direct chest isolation and less triceps, use the pec
  deck. Both are correct; the machine press adds a little more total load.
- **Cues:** full range, controlled, squeeze at peak contraction. Don't shrug the shoulders forward.

### C2. Chest-Supported Row (T-bar or seal row) — *mid-back, zero lower-back cost*
**3 × 10–12 · RPE 8–9 · Start: calibrate**
- **Why chest-supported specifically:** Tuesday's barbell row already taxed your lower back and
  spinal erectors. Lying against a pad removes them from the equation entirely, so the mid-back
  gets a full second dose without stacking spinal fatigue two days before your Lower session.
- **Cues:** chest stays pinned to the pad — if you're peeling off it, the weight is too heavy.
  Elbows drive back, squeeze the shoulder blades together, full stretch at the front.

## D. Tri-set — *side delts + biceps + triceps*
Do **D1 → D2 → D3 back to back, then rest 90 s. 3 rounds.** Three unrelated small muscles, so
they don't interfere; running them together fits 9 sets into ~9 minutes.

### D1. Dumbbell Lateral Raise — *side delts*
**3 × 12–20 · RPE 9 · Start: 2 × 6–8 kg**
- **Why again this week:** side delt width is one of the highest-visual-return developments
  available, and the muscle is small and recovers fast — it tolerates and benefits from twice-weekly
  work. Dumbbells here rather than Monday's cable simply for a different strength curve.
- **Cues:** lead with the elbow, raise to shoulder height, no higher. Minimal swing. Slow negative.
  **Go lighter than your ego wants** — this is the most commonly over-loaded exercise in any gym.

### D2. EZ-Bar Curl — *biceps, heavier than Tuesday*
**2 × 10–12 · RPE 9 · Start: ~20 kg**
- **Why EZ-bar:** the angled grip reduces wrist strain compared to a straight bar while letting you
  load both arms together — heavier than Tuesday's incline dumbbell curls. Tuesday trained the
  biceps under stretch; this trains them under load. Both are useful.
- **Cues:** elbows pinned to your sides, no swinging or leaning back. Full extension at the bottom.

### D3. Cable Overhead Triceps Extension — *long head, second dose*
**2 × 10–15 · RPE 9 · Start: calibrate**
- **Why overhead again:** the triceps long head is the largest of the three heads and only gets
  fully stretched with the arm overhead. It's worth hitting twice weekly, and the cable keeps
  tension constant through the whole range.
- **Cues:** elbows forward and fixed, deep stretch behind the head, full lockout, squeeze.

## Session summary

| | Exercise | Sets × Reps | RPE | Rest | Start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Weighted Dip *(or Incline BB Press)* | 3 × 6–10 | 8 | 2 min | bodyweight |
| B | Lat Pulldown (wide pronated) | 3 × 8–12 | 8 | 2 min | calibrate |
| C1 | Machine Chest Press / Pec Deck | 3 × 10–15 | 9 | superset | calibrate |
| C2 | Chest-Supported Row | 3 × 10–12 | 8–9 | 90 s | calibrate |
| D1 | DB Lateral Raise | 3 × 12–20 | 9 | tri-set | 2 × 6–8 kg |
| D2 | EZ-Bar Curl | 2 × 10–12 | 9 | tri-set | ~20 kg |
| D3 | Cable Overhead Triceps Ext | 2 × 10–15 | 9 | 90 s | calibrate |

> **Why the arms only get 2 sets each here.** Across the week your biceps and triceps already
> accumulate 15–16 counted sets through spillover — every row, pulldown, press and dip works them
> hard as assistants. A third direct set would add fatigue that your legs, the actual priority,
> have a stronger claim on. This is the volume budget being spent deliberately.

**Short on time?** Drop one set of C1 and one of C2. Never cut A or B — they carry the session's load.

**Bad sleep (< 6 h) or readiness ≤ 2/5?** Drop the last set of everything, subtract 1 from every
RPE target. See [Autoregulation](00-Program-Overview.md#6-autoregulation--mandatory-not-optional).
